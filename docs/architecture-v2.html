Data in, one gate out — v2: n8n news layer

News ingestion moves out of the Python worker into a self-hosted n8n automation layer. Every trade still passes through a single AI + risk gate before it becomes a journaled signal. The worker goes back to one job: turn prices into strategy candidates.

04 · The one gate — every trade passes here
gateCandidate() strategy candidate → AI validate → risk engine → journaled Signal

Risk engine enforces 1:2 R:R, daily-loss & drawdown limits, and the ±30-min HIGH-impact news blackout — fed entirely by the rows n8n now writes. Reject reasons are journaled too.

▲ What changed in v2

  • News ingestion runs in n8n, not the Python worker
  • New /analyze/news-summary endpoint — AI tags impact, respects the toggle
  • n8n gets its own Postgres DB; writes NewsEvent in the trading DB
  • Workflows exported as JSON & version-controlled

● What stayed (the load-bearing parts)

  • The single AI + risk gate — every trade still passes it
  • NewsEvent schema & the news-blackout check
  • Strategy runner, candle & indicator loops
  • The provider toggle as the one source of truth for AI

✕ What retires

  • services/data/news_fetcher.py
  • forexfactory + alpha_vantage worker loops
  • Removed only after n8n is verified writing rows
  • Worker → back to one job: prices → candidates
